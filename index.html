About

A bit about me

I'm Lautaro — a multidisciplinary, creative full-stack developer based in El Bolsón, Río Negro, Argentina. I specialise in building modern, attractive websites that are intuitive and easy to use, and I'm just as comfortable designing the interface as wiring up the backend behind it.

My path here is a bit unusual: a six-year technical secondary degree in electrical and electronic engineering, a stretch of aeronautical engineering at the UTN, and then an intensive full-stack bootcamp at Henry that pointed all of it at the web. Add an internship in Chile and ten months freelancing for clients across Argentina, and that's six years building for the web.

Earlier this year I founded Web Factory Studio, offering web design and native apps to the Latin American market. The technical work went well; the market wasn't quite ready. After several months I decided to wind it down.

What I took from running my own studio — scoping projects, talking to clients, shipping under real constraints — is what I want to bring into a team now. I'm looking for a full-stack role where I can focus on building product, not chasing leads.
